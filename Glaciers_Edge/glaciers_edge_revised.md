# Glacier’s Edge

*A Daggerheart one-shot for three level 3 characters | Revised edition*

# At a Glance

**Party:** 3 PCs, level 3 (Tier 2). **Runtime:** about 3 to 4 hours. **Battle Points per fight:** 11 (3 per PC + 2).

**Premise:** A frontier mining town sits at the foot of a glacier that is really a prison for an ancient ice elemental. Decades of mining have weakened it. Four factions each push a different answer, and the party can pick one, mix them, or invent their own. A countdown (the Thaw) keeps the open investigation under pressure, and the session ends with the monster breaking free.

**Session shape:** Opening scene, open investigation (the Thaw ticks as the party moves), then the Finale. Run three or four of the optional encounters and always run the Finale.

> **What changed in this revision**
>
> - **The Thaw:** a Consequence Countdown with scheduled events, so the sandbox has pressure without railroading.
> - **Clue Web:** every essential clue can be found in at least two places.
> - **Daggerheart conversion:** enemies and weapons now use Difficulty, thresholds, Minions, Reaction Rolls, and Fear moves. D&D-style armor, Constitution and Athletics checks are gone.
> - **Rime Armor and phases:** the Frozen Tyrant is a three-phase Solo whose defenses are removed by the weapons the party gathered, so prep matters.
> - **Finale chapter:** four endings, each with its own Progress Countdown and epilogue, plus Ally Tokens that reward winning people over.
> - **Location Fear options:** every location has a 1 Fear and a 2 Fear move for the GM to spend.
> - **New material:** the mayor’s clone now comes from Bones and Clones, the Guild’s mercenaries have stats, Appendix C holds four new handouts, and Appendix D lists the named NPCs with a memorable hook for each.
> - **Gaps filled:** the Forward Base, Compact Base, and Prison now have content. The Frostbane Chains and Sunfire Lances are now a real choice. The succubus’s soul-binding hooks into Daggerheart death moves.

# Running the Session

## Opening Scene

Start in motion. The party is in the market when the first big tremor hits. A bell clangs, cracks run through the cobbles, and the guards shout that the town is under lockdown. A miner staggers out of the glacier road, pushes a humming shard of frostglass into a PC’s hands, and gasps, “It’s waking. Find Frostmere.” The shard leads them to Neb Frostmere.

**Table question:** before play, ask each player which faction (or NPC) in town owes their character a favor, or holds a grudge. Use the answers to place hooks.

## The Thaw (Consequence Countdown, 10)

Put a 10-step Consequence Countdown on the table called **The Thaw**. Tick it when:

- the party finishes a scene at a location and moves on, or

- a PC rolls with Fear and fails.

Neb’s watch shows the current tick. If the party doesn’t have it, describe tremors and falling temperatures instead.

| **Tick** | **What happens**                                                                                                                                                                                     |
|----------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 2        | **First incident.** Ice elementals burst into the street (Encounter A).                                                                                                                              |
| 4        | **Lockdown tightens.** The Guard starts arresting people on sight (Encounter B, or arrest at Town Hall).                                                                                             |
| 6        | **Frost spreads.** The north streets freeze and shops close. Starting a scene in the north end costs 1 Stress unless warmed. Resting is no longer safe.                                              |
| 8        | **Point of no return.** Neb moves the charges to the glacier (unless he was talked down). The Church starts handing out its purple potions. The Merchants line up mercenaries at the glacier mouth. |
| 10       | **The Tyrant breaks free.** Go to the Finale.                                                                                                                                                        |

**Pacing:** if the party is moving fast, add a tick for dawdling. If they are stuck, skip one. Aim to start the Finale around the 2.5 hour mark.

# Background

## The Conflict

The town of Glacier’s Edge was built next to a huge glacier. The glacier is an icy prison that holds an ice elemental.

### The Monster

An ancient elemental force, a primordial being, was imprisoned in the glacier generations ago. It is currently frozen but thawing because of decades of mining damage. Where the original named it only as the monster or the ice tyrant, this edition calls it **the Frozen Tyrant**.

**Physical description (when freed):**

- A towering humanoid of living ice and ancient malice.

- Partially translucent, with veins of frostglass running through its body.

- Exhales blizzards and freezes anything it touches.

- Regenerates when near ice or cold.

**Abilities:** freezing aura (environmental hazard), summons ice elementals, creates ice walls and terrain, freezing breath, enhanced strength and durability.

**Weaknesses (discoverable through investigation) and what they do at the table:**

| **Weakness**           | **Mechanical effect**                                                                                                                      |
|------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Fire and heat          | Heartfire Braziers and Sunfire Lances. Braziers stop its regeneration and make it mark Stress. Lances remove Rime Armor and mark extra HP. |
| Sonic vibrations       | The Shattering Bell cracks its Rime Armor and shatters its ice constructs. In Phase 3, sonic hits mark an extra HP.                        |
| Frostglass weapons     | A weapon with a Frostglass Edge ignores Rime Armor’s damage gate.                                                                          |
| Binding rituals        | Enables the Bind ending, and a once-per-fight Binding Word that makes it lose a spotlight.                                                 |
| Its arrogance and rage | A successful taunt (Presence roll) forces its next spotlight onto the taunter, which sets up Spear of Ice dodges and positioning.          |

## The Town of Glacier’s Edge

A frontier mining town built at the base of an ancient glacier, about 80 to 100 years old. Population: 3,000 to 5,000.

### Primary Industries

- **Frostglass mining (main economy):** magical slabs of crystal, hard as steel, that store magical energy and are essential for high-level enchantments. The only known source in the region.

- **Ice crystal mining:** magical crystals that stay cold while they hold energy and can be refilled with magic.

### History

- **80 to 100 years ago:** prospectors discovered frostglass in the glacier and ignored warnings from the indigenous people and ancient markers. The glacier is a prison containing an ancient monster.

- **The founding sin:** the original founders (including the current noble’s great-grandfather) discovered that mining into the glacier weakened the prison but released valuable frostglass. They chose profit over safety.

- **The cover-up:** the noble family has known the truth for generations and suppressed it to protect their wealth and power. Each generation hoped the monster wouldn’t awaken on their watch.

- **Five years ago, the Deep Delve:** the Merchant Consortium funded aggressive deep mining that breached a critical seal. They saw the warning signs and pushed on for profit. This started the final countdown.

- **The past year:** tremors and strange sounds from the glacier, miners reporting visions in the ice, small ice elementals appearing, unnatural temperature drops, miners going mad or disappearing, and leadership covering it all up as “mining accidents.”

# The Factions

Each faction has a belief, a want, something to offer, and a plan for the finale. None of them is simply right.

## The Miners Compact

The Compact believes the town needs the glacier and the mines to survive, and isn’t sure the glacier will survive without the monster. Their plan is to dominate the monster, control it, and keep it alive. Neb’s son Aldric ran away to join them to make up for what his ancestors did.

- **Wants:** to Bind the Tyrant. **Offers:** the Binding tome, Aldric as a ritualist, sonic picks, and local guides. **Asks:** proof the party won’t sell them to the Church or the Merchants.

- **The cost:** a bound Tyrant is a prisoner with a long memory.

## The Noble’s Demolition Plan

Neb Frostmere is the great-great-grandson of Cole Frostmere, who found the mines. He inherited the mine and its wealth and was warned of the dangers. For generations his family covered up the truth. Now he is fed up and ready to act.

Neb has a large stock of alchemical explosives. Set off in the glacier, they will collapse it onto the monster and bury it forever. They will also destroy the town’s economic foundation and about half its buildings. He is willing to make the “necessary sacrifices” and believes this is the only certain solution.

- **Wants:** Aldric safe and the monster buried. **Offers:** the countdown watch, a key to the charges, and one frostglass slab. **The cost:** half the town.

## The Merchants Guild

The merchants are driven by the hoard of money the mines have generated. They have collected as many weapons as they could that might work on the monster, and they believe it can be killed if the right people stay in town.

- **Wants:** to keep the frostglass flowing and, ideally, chain the Tyrant to harvest monster parts again and again while it regenerates. **Offers:** the four Frostbane Chains, in exchange for a sworn favor or a signed Harvest Charter. **The cost:** the Tyrant’s endless agony.

## The Meltwater Church

The Church believes the monster is divine punishment for the town’s greed and corruption. They want to help it destroy the town so it can be “reborn pure.” They are connected to the town guard and are trying to keep people in town so they will suffer the consequences of their actions.

- **Wants:** the Tyrant free and the town punished. **Secretly:** their leader is a succubus collecting souls to cover a botched quota. **Offers:** nothing willingly, but their cathedral’s bell is a Shattering Bell, and their purple potion is their weapon.

# Locations

Each location lists what’s there and what it can give the party. Moving on from a location ticks the Thaw, so the party’s route is a real choice.

Each location also has two **Spend Fear** options: a 1 Fear complication and a 2 Fear escalation. Use them when the scene needs a push, or when a roll with Fear gives you the opening.

## Neb Frostmere’s Glass House

Neb lives in a house made mostly of frostglass. It is extremely expensive and a sign of his wealth. When the party first meets him he is frantic about getting his son back. When pressed, he reveals his plan to destroy the glacier at the risk of the whole town. He doesn’t think this is a big deal, but it is.

- **Talking him down:** he will stand down only if the party promises to take care of the awakening monster another way (Presence roll, Difficulty 13, or 15 if they have no concrete plan). In return he gives them his watch, which shows the Thaw.

- **The charges:** all the Glacierbreaker charges are kept at his third vacation home in the city. (The second one blew up because they are so volatile.) If the party wants them as a last resort, he gives them the key.

- **Retrieving them:** a Finesse or Instinct roll (Difficulty 13) to move them safely. A failure with Fear sets one off early: 2d6+2 physical to everyone nearby (Agility Reaction Roll, Difficulty 13, for half).

- **Frostglass slab:** he can spare one, which is enough for a Frostglass Edge.

- **A free sonic hint:** the house hums and rings when the glacier shakes.

- **If ignored:** at Tick 8 he moves the charges to the glacier himself.

**Spend Fear:**

- **1 Fear, The Glass Sings:** a tremor makes the whole house ring like a struck bell. Each PC inside makes an Instinct Reaction Roll (Difficulty 13) or marks 1 Stress. This also hands them the sonic clue.
- **2 Fear, Desk Sample:** a Glacierbreaker grenade Neb left on his desk starts to hiss. The PCs get one action to deal with it: defuse it (Finesse, Difficulty 13) or throw it outside (Agility, Difficulty 12). Otherwise it goes off: 2d6+2 physical to everything within Very Close (Agility Reaction Roll, Difficulty 13, for half), a frostglass wall shatters, and Neb panics and won’t talk until calmed (Presence, Difficulty 13).

## The Spider’s Inn / Tavern

The Spider’s Inn is the hiding place of the Miners Compact. The Church, which controls most of the town, doesn’t look kindly on them. The innkeeper, Gus, is gruff and surly and doesn’t like talking to people. There is also an old storyteller, Old Wick, who can explain more about the monster.

- **Entering the cellar:** Gus allows it only if the party knows the password and shows they mean the Compact no ill will (Presence roll, Difficulty 14, with advantage if they bring news of Aldric).

- **The password is “Lamplight.”** It comes from the first line of the Compact’s flyer (“In corners where the lamplight dies”), so sharp players may guess it. Gus still wants to see that they mean no harm.

- **Where the password comes from:** Neb (if the party promises to bring Aldric home), a prisoner in the Town Prison, or a drunk Cultist who lets it slip.

- **Old Wick, the storyteller:** tells the legend as a tall tale. Each truth in it is a clue: the imprisonment, the sonic weakness (the bell song), and the Hell-bound dead.

- **Optional Encounter D, tavern brawl:** a few Meltwater Church members come in drunk and start harassing Gus. Use bar stools and the cellar door as terrain. Drunk Cultists are loose with their tongues, which is how the party learns about the potion.

**Spend Fear:**

- **1 Fear, Gus Clams Up:** Gus decides he doesn’t like the PCs’ faces. The next roll to get into the cellar has Disadvantage unless they do him a favor first, such as throwing out a drunk or fixing the stuck cellar door.
- **2 Fear, Church Informant:** someone in the common room is a Church spy, and they slip out. Start a Consequence Countdown (3) that ticks each time the PCs take a significant action here. At 0, a Town Guard Squad and 3 Cult Zealots arrive to search the inn, and the Compact seals the cellar for the rest of the scene. Catching the spy before they leave (Instinct to spot them, Difficulty 14) stops it.

## The City Library

The library isn’t large. This isn’t a city of knowledge, so it mostly has fiction and children’s books. Look closer, though, and the truth is hidden in them.

- **The search:** an Instinct or Knowledge roll (Difficulty 12) finds which tales are true. Success reveals the Tyrant’s abilities and weaknesses. A success with Hope also reveals the bell and the binding ritual.

- **Time:** the search takes a scene, so it ticks the Thaw. That makes it a real trade-off against other locations.

- **The children’s corner:** among the picture books is *Goodnight, Glacier’s Edge*, a bedtime book in which a sleepy child says goodnight to each landmark in town: the fountain, the bell, the glass house, the mine. A bookplate inside reads “Donated by the Frostmere Family.” The book ends on the line “Goodnight moon, goodnight mayor,” and that page is dog-eared, with “for emergencies only” penciled in the margin. Any PC who flips through it finds this, no roll needed. It only makes sense once the party suspects the mayor is a clone.

**Spend Fear:**

- **1 Fear, Lost in the Stacks:** the shelves are a mess and the catalog is a lie. The search ticks the Thaw one extra time, unless a PC marks a Stress to push through.
- **2 Fear, Torn Pages:** someone got here first. The pages about the bell and the binding ritual have been torn out, and a Cult Zealot is slipping out the back with them. Run a short chase (Agility, Difficulty 13) or a fight with 3 Cult Zealots (1 Minion group) to get them back. If the Zealot escapes, the pages end up at the Chapel.

## City’s Central Grass Area

The central grass area has the Fountain of Mixed Blessings, which can only be used once by any person who visits. When the party arrives, the fountain’s keeper, Orm Alein, is being arrested by the town guard. This is where **Encounter B** happens.

- **The scene:** Sergeant Odo Trask and two squads of Guards, a panicking crowd, and the fountain in the middle. Intervening can be a fight, a Presence roll to talk the Guards down, or a distraction. Orm, if freed, becomes an ally (1 Ally Token) and can tell them what he knows about the fountain.

If the party bathes an item in the fountain, it gains a boon and a curse. Roll a d6:

| **d6** | **Boon**                                                                   | **Curse**                                                                                            |
|--------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1      | Warm to the touch: the carrier ignores Chill and cold hazards.             | Brittle: if the carrier rolls with Fear and fails, they mark an extra Stress.                        |
| 2      | Lucky: once per session, reroll a Hope die.                                | Jealous: advantage dice from allies don’t apply to the carrier.                                      |
| 3      | Quiet: advantage on rolls to move unseen.                                  | Heavy: Disadvantage on the carrier’s Agility Reaction Rolls.                                         |
| 4      | Bright: sheds light and shows hidden things.                               | Loud: it hums when the glacier shakes, and the GM may spend a Fear to make it do so at a bad moment. |
| 5      | Keen: +1 to damage once per scene.                                         | Hungry: it dulls after each fight unless the carrier marks 1 Stress.                                 |
| 6      | Reflective: once per session, turn a Reaction Roll failure into a success. | Vain: the carrier marks Stress when they fail a roll in front of an audience.                        |

**Spend Fear:**

- **1 Fear, Crowd Surge:** the panicking crowd shoves. One PC makes an Agility Reaction Roll (Difficulty 12) or is swept away from the group and ends up at Far range, separated for the next exchange.
- **2 Fear, The Fountain’s Whim:** the fountain froths over on its own and splashes an item a PC is carrying (your choice). Roll on the d6 table. The curse takes hold now, and the boon only wakes up the first time the PC spends a Hope with the item. Because the fountain works once per person, that PC can’t bathe anything else in it.

## Town Hall

Town Hall holds a very busy mayor, Mayor Dunmore, trying to coordinate the lockdown and get the city under control. He is secretly sympathetic to the Meltwater Church. He does **not** want the party to stop the monster. He wants all those rich slobs to pay for their undeserved, unsustainable way of life and for their lies against the people of his town.

- **He will look for reasons to arrest the party.** An arrest isn’t a failure state. It is the Prison Escape scene (see Town Prison).

- **The mayor is a clone.** There is a secret phrase, his sleeper word, that sends him into sleeper mode: **“Goodnight moon, goodnight mayor.”** When a PC speaks it, he goes still and the lockdown loses its head. For the rest of the session, Guard squads can be talked down with advantage, and the Guard counts as one Ally Token in the Finale.

- **Where the word is found:** see the Clue Web. The surest source is the order book at Bones and Clones, which made him.

**Spend Fear:**

- **1 Fear, Papers, Please:** a harried clerk demands to see lockdown papers. A PC makes a Presence roll (Difficulty 13) to bluff past. On a failure, the mayor learns the party is poking around, and the next Guard encounter starts hostile with no chance to talk first.
- **2 Fear, Trumped-Up Charges:** the mayor finds his excuse. A Guard Sergeant and one Town Guard Squad arrive with a warrant. The party can go quietly (run the Prison Escape) or resist. Resisting is a fight in the Town Hall, and the Thaw ticks once.

## Town Prison

The Town Prison has only three cells. Two are full of people the guards picked up off the street for breaking lockdown. The general attitude in town is that nobody wants to be here anymore and nobody likes the guards.

- **Who’s inside (suggested):** a Town Hall clerk who knows the mayor’s sleeper word, and a miner who knows the Compact’s password. Both will talk in exchange for a way out.

- **If the party is arrested:** they wake in Cell 3. Run a **Prison Escape** as a Progress Countdown (4). Each success on one of these ticks it: talk the turnkey down (Presence, Difficulty 13), pick the lock (Finesse, Difficulty 14), get the cellmates to riot (Presence, Difficulty 12), or crack the frostglass seam in the wall (Strength, Difficulty 13). A failure with Fear ticks the Thaw.

**Spend Fear:**

- **1 Fear, The Turnkey’s Rounds:** the turnkey comes by at the worst moment. During a Prison Escape, undo one tick of its countdown. During a visit, a frightened prisoner starts shouting, and the PCs must quiet them (Presence, Difficulty 12) before the guards come to look.
- **2 Fear, Transfer:** the guards move the prisoner the party needs (the clerk or the miner) to Town Hall. To reach them, the party either goes to Town Hall (a new scene, which ticks the Thaw) or ambushes the transfer in the street against one Town Guard Squad.

## Town Chapel (at the Cathedral)

The chapel is the head base of the Meltwater Church. When the adventurers enter, the cult members are brewing a big pot of purple potion. They say it’s a cold resistance potion, but on closer inspection it is a cold vulnerability potion that they plan to give to the town. The cult will not be happy if the party finds this out.

- **Inspecting the potion:** Instinct or Knowledge roll (Difficulty 13). Success reveals what it is. Destroying the pot prevents the Tick 8 potion handout.

- **The bell:** the bell above the church is a Shattering Bell. If it’s given to the blacksmith, he can make it into a weapon. The party can take it quietly or in the middle of **Encounter E**.

- **The Matriarch:** the Church is run by Mother Seraphine, a very persuasive woman who is a succubus in disguise. A spell has been cast on the Frozen Tyrant: any person he kills is bound to the Hells. She is trying to reap souls to make up for a botched quota.

- **Encounter E:** the Matriarch uses Charm and rallies Zealots. When she is badly hurt she vanishes in smoke and returns in the Finale.

**Spend Fear:**

- **1 Fear, A Warm Cup:** a smiling Zealot offers a PC a cup of the purple potion as hospitality. Refusing without offense takes a Presence roll (Difficulty 14). On a failure the cult turns cold, and Encounter E can start early. A PC who drinks marks 1 extra HP the first time they take magic damage from an ice creature.
- **2 Fear, The Matriarch’s Gaze:** the Matriarch appears and uses Charm on a PC without marking a Stress. A Charmed PC also tells her the most important thing the party has learned so far, and she acts on it, for example by moving the bell or warning the mayor.

## \[Secret\] Miners Compact Home Base (under the tavern)

When the party reaches the Compact, they find bookshelves of magical books and a group of wary, hunted miners. Aldric Frostmere is here.

- **The books:** the Binding tome (needed for the Bind ending and the Binding Word), notes on the Tyrant’s weaknesses, and notes on how the mayor’s clone works.

- **Aldric:** a nervous ritualist trying to make up for his family. He is an Ally Token in the Finale and the key to the Bind ending.

- **Shale, the Compact’s leader,** is wary and protective of their people. They’ll want proof the party won’t betray them.

**Spend Fear:**

- **1 Fear, Frayed Nerves:** a jumpy miner is sure one of the PCs is a Church spy and says so loudly. The next roll to win the Compact over has Disadvantage until the party proves itself.
- **2 Fear, Raid:** the Church has found the cellar. One Town Guard Squad and 3 Cult Zealots come down the stairs. Start a Progress Countdown (3) to get Aldric and the Binding tome out through the old mine tunnel. If the fight ends before it fills, the party loses one of the two (their choice): the tome burns, or Aldric is dragged to the Chapel.

## Merchant Guild Recruiting Office

The Merchants Guild is concerned with making a profit, and that is just about it. They worry that if the glacier or the monster is destroyed, they may not be able to make frostglass or ice crystals. If the monster is contained, they can harvest monster parts from it again and again and let it regenerate. That would make the mines safer, but would cause the monster great agony.

- **The offer:** Recruiting Officer Mallick offers the Frostbane Chains in exchange for a sworn favor (escort a shipment, scare the Compact) or a signed Harvest Charter that promises the Tyrant to them alive.

- **The catch:** if the party breaks the Charter, the Merchants’ mercenaries show up at the Finale on the other side: a Mercenary Captain and 3 Guild Mercenaries (1 Minion group). See Appendix A.

**Spend Fear:**

- **1 Fear, The Fine Print:** Recruiting Officer Mallick slips in an extra clause. Whoever signs owes the Guild one more favor than they thought. Call it in at the worst possible moment.
- **2 Fear, Rival Bidder:** someone else has made the Guild a better offer for the chains. The party must outbid them (Presence, Difficulty 15, or a second sworn favor). Otherwise the Guild sells two of the four Frostbane Chains elsewhere, and the party can only get the remaining two.

## Miners Compact Forward Base (at the entrance to the glacier)

This section was blank in the original. The original calls it the Miners Guild Forward Base, which is easy to confuse with the Merchants Guild, so this edition calls it the Compact’s forward base.

- **What’s here:** a ruined camp at the glacier mouth: unlit braziers, mining gear, sonic picks, and the first crack in the ice.

- **Staging:** the Finale takes place here. Any scene here before Tick 10 lets the party prepare the arena (place braziers, plant charges, set chains).

**Spend Fear:**

- **1 Fear, Crystal Bloom:** frozen energy seeps out of the crack. Place an Ice Crystal hazard within Close range of a PC.
- **2 Fear, It Stirs:** the shape in the ice twitches. Summon 3 Ice Elementals (1 Minion group), and undo one piece of the party’s prep here: a brazier tips into the snow, a charge’s fuse freezes, or a chain anchor cracks.

## ‘Tough Enough’ Blacksmith

When the party arrives, they find three townsfolk robbing the blacksmith. One is a townsman looking to take weapons to arm his friends so he can fight his way out of town. This is **Encounter C**, and it’s best resolved by talking (Presence, Difficulty 12) or by one decisive hit that makes the others reconsider.

The blacksmith, Big Tam, has one goal, which is to keep the town alive at any cost. He helps the party make mythical weapons from what they bring him. If the party knows about the monster’s weaknesses, he can build up to three Heartfire Braziers.

- **Chains:** he can turn Frostbane Chains into Sunfire Lances (2 chains per lance).

- **The bell:** he can turn the Shattering Bell into a weapon.

- **Frostglass Edge:** he can edge a weapon if given a frostglass slab.

- **Time:** one scene of crafting, which ticks the Thaw once, covers everything the party brings at the same time. Bring it all at once or pay for each trip.

**Spend Fear:**

- **1 Fear, Overworked Forge:** the forge can’t keep up. One item isn’t ready until after the party’s next scene, unless a PC works the bellows (Strength, Difficulty 12, and mark 1 Stress).
- **2 Fear, Light Fingers:** one of the Encounter C robbers got away with something, either a finished item or the frostglass slab. Their trail leads to the ‘Food n Stuff’ General Store, where the desperate townsfolk have it. Getting it back means a talk (Presence, Difficulty 13) or a fight.

## ‘Food n Stuff’ General Store

The general store holds a group of townsfolk who were fought back by the guard and are recouping here while they plan how to escape town.

- **What they want:** a way out. If the party calms them or opens a gate (Presence, Difficulty 13), they earn 1 Ally Token and a Heartfire Brazier’s worth of supplies.

**Spend Fear:**

- **1 Fear, A Desperate Ask:** a parent begs the party to smuggle their child out of town. Each PC who refuses marks 1 Stress. A PC who agrees has a new obligation to deal with before the Finale.
- **2 Fear, Rush the Gate:** the townsfolk decide to break for the South Gate right now. The party must talk them down (Presence, Difficulty 14) or go with them. If neither, the Guard crushes the attempt: the store’s Ally Token is lost and the Thaw ticks once.

## Bones and Clones People Repair

They will heal you up fully, clearing all HP and Stress, but their prices are incredibly high (a bag of gold per PC, or a favor). When the party gets there, there is a fire in the basement that will consume the building if left unchecked. This is **Encounter F**.

- **The Fire:** a Consequence Countdown (4). Each round it ticks once. The party needs 3 successes before it ends: haul water (Strength, Difficulty 12), smother it (Instinct, Difficulty 12), or evacuate patients (Presence, Difficulty 12). If it reaches 0, the building is lost and the clinic’s healing is gone.

- **The reward:** saving the building earns the clinic’s thanks, a free full heal, and 1 Ally Token.

- **The clinic’s secret: the mayor.** The real Mayor Dunmore died of glacier sickness two years ago. The Frostmere family quietly paid Bones and Clones for a copy to keep the cover-up running, with a sleeper word built in as a safety catch. The copy came out with all of the original’s grudges against the rich and none of his fear. The order, the payment, and the sleeper word (“Goodnight moon, goodnight mayor”) are written in the clinic’s **order book**, kept in the basement where the fire is.

- **Getting the order book:** grabbing it from the fire is an action (Agility, Difficulty 13, or mark 1 Stress from the heat). If the Fire countdown reaches 0 first, the book burns, and the party has to find the word through another source in the Clue Web. If the party saves the building, the grateful clinic staff can confirm the order themselves.

**Spend Fear:**

- **1 Fear, Flare-Up:** a jar of something flammable bursts. Tick the Fire countdown one extra time.
- **2 Fear, Something Wakes in the Basement:** the heat wakes a half-finished clone from its vat, confused and violent. Use the Guard Sergeant’s stats without Form Up or Sound the Whistle. It wears the mayor’s face, which is a strong hint that the mayor is a clone.

# The Finale: The Tyrant Breaks Free

When the Thaw reaches 10 (or the party moves on the glacier earlier), the scene moves to the Shattered Prison at the Forward Base. Open with the glacier itself: a hundred feet of ice splits down the middle, blue light pours out, and a hand the size of a cart closes over the ledge.

## Prep Pays Off

Everything the party did before the Finale should change this scene. Give them the benefit at the table.

| **What the party did**                            | **Finale effect**                                                                                                                                                       |
|---------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Talked Neb down and took the charges              | Charges and grenades are available. The Bury countdown starts at 2.                                                                                                     |
| Found the Binding tome or allied with the Compact | Once per fight, a PC can speak a **Binding Word** (Knowledge, Difficulty 15): the Tyrant loses its next spotlight. The Bind countdown starts at 2, and Aldric is present. |
| Got the chains from the Merchants                 | Up to 4 Frostbane Chains. Every chain made into a lance is one fewer chain.                                                                                             |
| Forged weapons                                    | Lances, the Bell, and the Frostglass Edge all strip Rime Armor.                                                                                                         |
| Built braziers                                    | Braziers hold ground against Chill, Regenerate, and Pylons.                                                                                                             |
| Destroyed or exposed the purple potion            | If the potion reached the crowd at Tick 8, the Whiteout starts with 2 ticks already marked.                                                                             |
| Exposed the Matriarch                             | Her Silver Tongue is off, and she doesn’t get a surprise appearance.                                                                                                    |
| Silenced the mayor’s clone                        | A Guard squad fights alongside the party (1 Ally Token).                                                                                                                |
| Broke the Harvest Charter                         | A Mercenary Captain and 3 Guild Mercenaries arrive to protect the Guild’s asset. To keep the fight at its budget, remove one Ice Pylon and one Elemental summon.           |

### Ally Tokens

Each ally the party earned (Orm, the store’s townsfolk, Aldric, the blacksmith, the Guard, the clinic) is 1 Ally Token, up to 3 total. Once per token during the Finale, a player can spend one to **grant advantage on a roll, clear 2 Stress, or ask the ally to take one hit** for a PC. This is how winning people over pays off at the climax.

## The Arena: The Shattered Prison

**Tier 2 environment.** Impulses: entomb the proud, freeze everything, and remind them how small they are.

- **Whiteout (Consequence Countdown, 6):** starts when the Tyrant breaks free. Tick it whenever you spend a Fear on a move. At 0, a blizzard blinds the arena: PCs can only see and target within Close, and ranged attacks are made with Disadvantage until a brazier is lit or the Bell is rung. A PC can push it back by one tick by tending a lit brazier (Instinct roll, Difficulty 12, as an action).

- **Falling Icicles (Fear move):** spend a Fear: choose a Far-range area. PCs there make an Agility Reaction Roll (Difficulty 13) or take 1d10+3 physical. Icicles that miss become difficult terrain.

- **Ice Crystal Clusters:** four clusters scattered across the floor (see Appendix A). Hit by anything, they explode. Players can use them on purpose.

- **Miner’s Scaffold:** a high platform with a winch. Good for ranged attacks, charge placement, and the Binding ritual. The Tyrant can knock it down.

- **The Fissure:** the central pit where the Tyrant stands in Phase 1. Anyone who falls in takes 2d6 physical and is Vulnerable until they climb out.

## The Tyrant’s Phases

| **Phase**         | **Trigger**          | **What changes**                                                                                                                                                                       |
|-------------------|----------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1\. The Thaw      | Start. HP 10 to 7.   | Encased: it can’t move and can’t use Relentless, Freezing Breath, or Flash Freeze. It fights with Pylons, Elementals, Spear of Ice, and Ice Prison. The party breaks Rime and sets up. |
| 2\. Breaking Free | It marks its 4th HP. | The ice shatters (Agility Reaction Roll, Difficulty 14, or 2d8+3 physical to all within Far). It gains Relentless (2), Freezing Breath, and Flash Freeze. The Whiteout ticks once.     |
| 3\. Cracked       | It marks its 7th HP. | Its frostglass heart is exposed. Thresholds drop by 3. Relentless becomes (3). Sonic hits mark an extra HP. Glacial Fist gains +1d10 damage.                                           |

## The Matriarch in the Finale

If she escaped, she arrives when the Tyrant enters Phase 2, or when the first PC makes a Death Move. She doesn’t fight at first. She **offers a Bargain**: a PC can choose Avoid Death, and she will accept it with a debt (the GM names one favor she’ll collect later). A PC who chooses **Blaze of Glory** and strikes the Tyrant’s last blow breaks the soul-binding for themselves and everyone the Tyrant has killed. If you want a second boss beat, she steps in with Charm and Cult Rally.

## Ending A: Bury It

**Requires:** the charges (Neb’s third house). **Feel:** a race and a gamble.

- **Progress Countdown (6): Place the Charges.** Each success on a relevant roll advances it by 1: climb the scaffold (Agility), set fuses (Finesse), read the cracks (Instinct), or hold the Tyrant in the Fissure with chains (Strength). It starts at 2 if the party brought Neb’s help.

- **Complications:** every failed roll ticks the Whiteout or lets the Tyrant act. The Tyrant tries to Drain Pylons and shatter the scaffold.

- **The finish:** when it completes, they light the fuses and run. Make a final chase of Agility Reaction Rolls (Difficulty 13) as the ice comes down.

- **Epilogue:** the Tyrant is entombed, the glacier is dead, and half the town with its economy. Neb watches it fall. The prison holds, but whatever it holds is still down there.

## Ending B: Bind It

**Requires:** the Binding tome and Aldric. **Feel:** a ritual under fire.

- **Progress Countdown (8): The Binding.** Attune three frostglass slabs around the Fissure (Knowledge, Difficulty 14), keep Aldric safe while he chants (Presence, Difficulty 13, to steady him), and stay alive. It starts at 2 with the Compact’s help.

- **Complications:** the Matriarch tries to Charm Aldric. The Tyrant uses Ice Prison on the ritualists and drains slabs it can reach.

- **Epilogue:** the Tyrant is bound. The Compact controls a living prison, and it listens. The town survives, and the cost is a god in chains who knows every name.

## Ending C: Kill It

**Requires:** the weapons. **Feel:** a pure boss fight, tuned by prep.

- **The shape of it:** break Rime, hit it, and keep the Pylons down. Use chains to pin it, braziers to stop its regeneration, and the Bell to open it up. Spotlight the players’ prep.

- **Epilogue:** the Tyrant shatters and frostglass pours out of the ground. The Merchants move in at once. If the party signed the Harvest Charter, its terms come due.

## Failure: Winter Falls

If the party isn’t ready when the Thaw hits 10, the Tyrant breaks out with Rime at full strength and the Whiteout starts 3 ticks in. The Church celebrates. If the party then loses, the epilogue is the town buried in white and the Matriarch’s ledger filling.

## Mixed Endings

Any combination works. Chain it, then bury it. Bind it, then kill it. Each completed objective gives its starting ticks and effects. Describe the combined epilogue by stacking the costs.

## Dials

- **Easier:** remove 1 Rime, drop its Difficulty to 14, or give the party an extra Ally Token.

- **Harder:** add an extra Ice Pylon, start the Whiteout 2 ticks in, or let the Matriarch fight alongside it.

## Running the Tyrant

- **Telegraph, then release.** Spear of Ice is the rhythm: charge, let the table react, then hurl. Give the players a beat to respond.

- **Phase 1:** use Legion of Ice, Summon Pylon, Ice Prison, and a charged Spear. Make the party want to break Rime and kill Pylons.

- **Phase 2:** spend Fear on Relentless and Flash Freeze instead of banking it. Let the Whiteout tick as you spend.

- **Phase 3:** it’s desperate and cracked. Hit hard, let the sonic and fire prep shine, and keep the Matriarch’s Bargain ready.

- **Rule of thumb:** a good hit on the Tyrant should feel like it matters. If thresholds feel too high in play, drop them by 2 or remove 1 Rime.

## The Sleeper Under the Ice (Old Wick’s Song)

*GM note: Old Wick sings this at the Spider’s Inn, and a children’s picture-book version sits in the City Library. Each verse is a clue: the prison, the mining, fire, the bell, frostglass, its pride, and the Hell-bound dead.*

> *Before the town, before the mine,*
> *Before the first fool drew a line,*
> *The old folk sang the frozen king*
> *To sleep beneath the glacier’s wing.*
>
> *They built his bed of blue and white,*
> *They bound him there with words of might,*
> *And warned: “Who digs into his tomb*
> *Unlocks the door and lets him loom.”*
>
> *He hates the hearth, he hates the flame,*
> *The kindled coal that speaks his name.*
> *Where braziers burn and embers glow,*
> *His wounds won’t close, his ice won’t grow.*
>
> *He hates the bell, he hates its song.*
> *One silver note, both loud and long,*
> *Will crack the armor on his chest*
> *And shake the frost that guards him best.*
>
> *So cut him with the glass he grew,*
> *His own heart’s crystal cuts him through.*
> *And mock him well, for he is proud:*
> *He’ll chase the one who laughs aloud.*
>
> *But heed the last verse, child, and weep:*
> *The souls he takes, he does not keep.*
> *Each one he kills goes down below,*
> *To fires the Hells have banked for slow.*
>
> *So speak the words and keep him bound,*
> *Or ring the bell and bring him down,*
> *But never dig where old folk said,*
> *Or you’ll be counted with the dead.*
>
> *(Old Wick taps his cane on the floor three times. “That’s the verse they don’t sing anymore.”)*

## Harvest Charter

*GM note: Recruiting Officer Mallick slides this across the desk at the Merchant Guild Recruiting Office. Let the players read it, and let them sign it if they want the chains. Keep the signed copy: breaking it brings the mercenaries to the Finale.*

> **MERCHANTS GUILD OF GLACIER’S EDGE**
> **HARVEST CHARTER No. 113**
>
> This Charter is entered into by the Merchants Guild (“the Guild”) and the undersigned (“the Contractor”).
>
> 1. **Loan of Equipment.** The Guild lends the Contractor up to four (4) Frostbane Chains (“the Equipment”). The Equipment remains Guild property at all times. Equipment that is melted, reforged, or otherwise made into “not chains” will be billed at full replacement value.
> 2. **The Asset.** The Contractor agrees that the entity beneath the glacier (“the Asset”) shall be delivered to the Guild ALIVE, restrained, and harvestable.
> 3. **Harvest Rights.** All parts, fluids, frostglass, and byproducts of the Asset belong to the Guild, now and in perpetuity. The Asset’s regeneration is the Asset’s own concern.
> 4. **Prohibited Outcomes.** The Contractor shall not bury the Asset, kill the Asset, or bind the Asset on behalf of any other party. Any such outcome is a Breach.
> 5. **Breach.** In the event of Breach, the Guild may protect its Asset by any means, including the full force of Guild Security.
> 6. **Compensation.** A share of every harvest, payable upon harvest, as determined by the Guild.
> 7. **Continuity.** Should the Contractor die in the performance of this Charter, all obligations pass to the Contractor’s estate, heirs, and soul, where applicable.
>
> Signed: ______________________ (Contractor)
>
> Witnessed: *Recruiting Officer Mallick*
>
> <small>The Guild accepts no liability for glaciers, elementals, divine punishment, or Acts of Tyrant.</small>

# Appendix D: Named NPCs

Each NPC has a **hook**: a habit, look, or line to repeat every time they appear. Use it two or three times and the players will remember who they are.

- **Neb Frostmere** (noble mine owner, Glass House). Wants Aldric home and the monster buried, whatever it costs the town. **Hook:** he keeps snapping open his frostglass pocket watch to check the time, and he calls the town’s destruction “necessary sacrifices.”
- **Aldric Frostmere** (Neb’s runaway son, Compact Home Base). A nervous ritualist trying to pay back his family’s debt. **Hook:** ink-stained fingers, and he apologizes for his family before anyone accuses him: “I’m sorry. We’re sorry.”
- **Cole Frostmere** (the founder, long dead). Chose profit over the warnings. **Hook:** his portrait hangs in the Glass House, with eyes of real frostglass that seem to follow the party, and the family motto beneath reads “Profit Before Prudence.”
- **Gus** (innkeeper, Spider’s Inn). Gruff gatekeeper to the Compact’s cellar. **Hook:** answers in one word whenever he can, and keeps a fat pet spider named Karen on the bar.
- **Old Wick** (storyteller, Spider’s Inn). Tells the legend as a tall tale. **Hook:** opens every story with “Ever hear the one about…?” and taps his cane on the floor three times when he finishes.
- **Shale** (leader of the Miners Compact). Wary and protective. **Hook:** never takes off their battered mining helmet with its candle stub, and talks in mining terms (“that plan won’t hold weight,” “you’re digging in the wrong seam”).
- **Orm Alein** (fountain keeper, Central Grass Area). Freed in Encounter B, he explains the fountain. **Hook:** always slightly damp, and he flips a coin before every answer: “Heads, a blessing. Tails, a curse.”
- **Mayor Dunmore** (mayor and secret clone, Town Hall). Runs the lockdown and wants the rich to pay. **Hook:** says “for the good of the town” constantly, and his gestures repeat exactly, as if he were wound up. Players who notice the repetition are getting a clue.
- **Sergeant Odo Trask** (Guard Sergeant). Enforces a lockdown he doesn’t believe in. **Hook:** sighs heavily before every order, mutters “I don’t make the rules,” and fiddles with the whistle on his chest.
- **Mother Seraphine** (the Matriarch, Town Chapel). A succubus filling a soul quota. **Hook:** she is always warm in the cold, smells of cinnamon and smoke, and calls everyone “little lamb.”
- **Vera Stone** (grieving Church member, the “V.S.” on the flyer). Lost her daughter in the mines and was paid to stay quiet. **Hook:** carries her daughter’s unlit mining lamp everywhere and says, “They paid me to be silent.” If the party shows her the ledger, she can turn on the Church.
- **Recruiting Officer Mallick** (Merchants Guild). Offers the Frostbane Chains for a favor or a signed Charter. **Hook:** a gold tooth, a quill always in hand, and “Sign here, initial there.”
- **Big Tam** (blacksmith, ‘Tough Enough’). Wants the town alive and forges the mythical weapons. **Hook:** a huge, gentle man who hums while he hammers and knocks on everything the party brings in, asking, “Tough enough?”
- **The Frozen Tyrant** (the ice elemental). Breaks free in the Finale. **Hook:** speaks in a chorus of every miner it has killed, and calls the townsfolk “little thieves.”
