# Places in Glacier's Edge

A frontier mining town at the foot of a great glacier. The town is under lockdown, and the guard has closed the South Gate.

See `glaciers_edge_map.png` (or `glaciers_edge_map.svg`) for the town map.

## North End

| Place | What you know about it |
|---|---|
| **Forward Base** | A mining camp where the Glacier Road meets the ice, at the glacier entrance. |
| **Neb Glass's Glass House** | The home of Neb Glass, whose family owns the mines. It's built almost entirely of frostglass, so you can't miss it. |
| **'Tough Enough' Blacksmith** | The town smithy. If it can be forged, the smith can probably forge it. |
| **Bones and Clones People Repair** | The town's healers. They're very good and very expensive. |

## Town Center

| Place | What you know about it |
|---|---|
| **Town Hall** | The mayor's seat, and the headquarters of the lockdown. |
| **Town Chapel** (at the Cathedral) | Home of the Meltwater Church. Its great bell can be heard across town. |
| **Central Grass Area** | The town green, with the Fountain of Mixed Blessings at its center. Each person may only use the fountain once. |
| **Town Prison** | A small jail with three cells. Lately the guard has been filling them with lockdown breakers. |
| **City Library** | A small library, mostly fiction and children's books. |

## Market District

| Place | What you know about it |
|---|---|
| **The Market** | The town's main square, where the Glacier Road comes down into town. |
| **The Spider's Inn** | A tavern with a spider on its sign. The innkeeper, Karen, is gruff and doesn't like talking to people. |
| **Merchants Guild Recruiting Office** | The Guild is hiring fighters, scouts, and anyone who can handle the cold. |
| **'Food n Stuff' General Store** | The general store, crowded these days with townsfolk who can't get out. |
