## Aldrics Letter.docx (Chad's letter to Neb)

Father,

If you're reading this, you've discovered I'm gone. Don't waste time looking for me in the usual places.

You taught me that nobility means responsibility. That our family's wealth comes with obligation. But you never told me the TRUTH - that our fortune is built on a lie. That Cole Glass knew. That every Glass since has known. That you've always known.

You're planning to bury it. Bury the threat, bury half the town, bury me if I'm in the way. I know about the charges. I know about the "necessary sacrifices."

There's another way.

I've found miners who've seen what's down there. They understand what it is. It's not just a monster, Father. It's power. Ancient power. And power can be directed. Controlled. Bound. This town needs that glacier.

Yes, the ritual has a cost. But what doesn't? Your plan costs half the town and destroys everything that makes this place worth saving. The economy. The mines. Our family's entire legacy turned to rubble and guilt.

At least my way offers a future. And maybe it's how a Glass finally pays back what we took.

I'm not asking for your blessing. I'm not even asking for your understanding. I'm just asking that when you find this, you remember that I'm doing what YOU taught me - protecting people at any cost.

Even if that cost is me.

Your son,
Chad

## Frostbound Compact Flyer.docx (Miners Compact)

*In corners where the lamplight dies, Where crystal walls reflect our eyes, The answer waits for those who see— What's bound can serve, what sleeps can be.*

They tell you it cannot be stopped. They tell you the town must choose: death or poverty. They tell you there is no third path.

They are wrong.

What if the threat could become the solution? What if control was possible? What if those willing to sacrifice could save everyone?

Not all prisons are meant to hold forever. Not all chains are meant to bind. Not all power is meant to destroy.

Some of us have seen what lies beneath. Some of us have touched the ancient cold. Some of us know there is another way.

If you seek answers they don't want you to find:

*Where the Spider spins above the door,*
*Where a surly host pours ale, no more,*
*Beneath the boards, in cellar night,*
*Bring courage, wit, and no Church-light.*

*Knock thrice upon the cellar door,*
*Speak the word that miners bore:*
*The price of knowing what we've seen,*
*Is knowing what you might have been.*

The word is kept by those who paid for it. Some wait in cells. Some live behind glass. The brave will find it. The worthy will understand.

We do not promise safety. We do not promise comfort. We promise only purpose, and the chance to save what they would destroy.

The binding requires sacrifice. The ritual requires will. The future requires YOU.

We look forward to you finding us. - The Miners Compact
